What's your best childhood vacation memory? When you plan for vacations now, do you keep those things in mind?

Friends of mine are planning a trip to San Miguel de Allende.  When I was 8 years old, the age of my youngest son now, we spent a summer in San Miguel de Allende. I started making a list of the things I remembered and was surprised at what a momentous summer that was.

- I learned how to play marbles in the dirt in the main square.
- I got to hold a pet monkey.
- I saw a tortilla making machine for the first time.
- We ate lunch in a "restaurant" that was someone's garage. My mom told me that the family probably did not get very much to eat but fed it to us to get money to live.
- My dad almost got pick pocketed and while he was busy yelling at the pickpocketer, my younger sister and I got off the bus by ourselves. It was our stop.
- We got home made, cloth dolls that were gorgeous.
- I learned how to knit.
- We had absolutely awesome fruit salads. And they counted a meal, not dessert.
- I got to drink coke at every meal because my dad wouldn't let us drink the water.
- We made friends.

What do you remember?
